**Before you start:** this Level 3 course deals with death and loss. If you are newly bereaved, seriously ill, or having thoughts of dying or harming yourself, please save the course for another time and see [Get help now](https://daynentang.com/en/get-help/) straight away.

**This week:** read the story of Zhuangzi near death in chapter XXXII slowly, and reread the "fire" sentence from Week 4. Sit for 30–40 minutes each day. Rate the six habits you have practised, keep two, and write a plan for the next four weeks.

**Measurable goal:** sit for 30–40 minutes on at least 5 of 7 days; rate 6 habits (0–10), choose 2 to keep, and write a 4-week plan with specific minutes.

## 1. Traditional teaching

[Traditional teaching] Chapter XXXII of the *Zhuangzi*, "Lieh Yü-k'ou", tells of the time when Zhuangzi was near death. His disciples wanted to give him a grand funeral.

::: kinh zhuangzi-giles nguyen-van | chapter XXXII, when Zhuangzi was about to die
When Chuang Tzŭ was about to die, his disciples expressed a wish to give him a splendid funeral. But Chuang Tzŭ said, "With Heaven and Earth for my coffin and shell; with the sun, moon, and stars as my burial regalia; and with all creation to escort me to the grave,—are not my funeral paraphernalia ready to hand?"
:::

::: kinh zhuangzi-giles nguyen-van | chapter III, last sentence (reread from Week 4)
The fuel is consumed, but the fire may be transmitted, and we know not that it comes to an end.
:::

### Reading it in context

- [Inference] The story is about not clinging to display, even in death. It is not advice on how to arrange a funeral for a loved one. Funerals in each family follow custom, faith and the wishes of the person who has died; the course does not discuss this.
- The story goes on: the disciples fear the kites will eat their master's body, and Zhuangzi answers with a line about kites above and ants below. The course does not quote that part. It also leaves out the English poem Giles prints alongside.

## 2. What science finds

- [Verified] Mindfulness meditation programmes reduce anxiety and depression by a small-to-moderate amount (47 trials, 3,515 people), no more than other active treatments. (Goyal et al. 2014)
- [Verified] A critical review notes that research on mindfulness and meditation still has many weaknesses: no agreed definition, many studies relying on self-report, and adverse effects not monitored enough. (Van Dam et al. 2018)
- No study has evaluated this course directly. Rating yourself at the end of the course is to learn what suits you, not scientific evidence.

## 3. Practice for adults

About 40 minutes a day; on the first day add 15 minutes to rate and plan.

1. **Slow reading (3 minutes).** Read both passages. Pause at "ready to hand".
2. **Sit for 30–40 minutes.** Choose the way of sitting that suited you best in earlier weeks: noticing change (Week 1), looking at nature (Week 2), scanning the body (Week 3), sitting or slow walking (Week 4).
3. **Back to the ground (2 minutes).** Open your eyes, put your feet on the floor, name three things before you stand up.
4. **Rate six habits (first day, 10 minutes).** Give each habit 0–10 points: how much you managed it, and how much it helped.
    - Noting one "change" from the day (Week 1)
    - Noting one change in nature (Week 2)
    - Noting one small change in the body that needs no fixing (Week 3)
    - One act of "passing on the fire" (Week 4)
    - One moment of "not demanding" (Week 5)
    - The 30–40-minute sitting
5. **Keep two, plan four weeks (first day, 5 minutes).** Choose the two habits with the highest scores. Write: when in the day, how many minutes, how many days a week. After the course, it is best to bring the sitting back to 20–30 minutes a day.
6. **The remaining days:** follow the plan you have written and tick it off.

## 4. Kids' corner 6+

**"The seed circle"** (5–10 minutes, with an adult)

1. Take a few mung beans or pumpkin seeds. With the child, draw a four-step circle: seed, sprout, plant, flower with seeds.
2. Put a real seed on the "seed" step of the paper.
3. Ask: "Where do new seeds come from? What can grow from a new seed?"
4. Finish with: "The circle keeps turning. A seed becomes a plant, and the plant gives seeds again."
5. If you like, plant a seed in a cup of damp soil and water it together every day.

Young children must not put seeds in their mouth, nose or ears. Wash hands after playing.

## 5. Stop practising if

- You feel panic, have traumatic flashbacks, a feeling that you or the world are "not real" that lasts, cannot sleep for several nights, or have thoughts of harming yourself or of dying.
- Thoughts about your own death or the death of loved ones keep coming back during the day and are hard to let go of, even after the course has ended.
- You find yourself more distant from your family, or less interested in work or daily life. Then pause the lessons on life and death, go back to the lessons in [Taoism 1](/en/courses/taoism-1/), and talk to someone you trust.
- If you have a history of psychosis, bipolar disorder or severe PTSD, ask a doctor before sitting for more than 20 minutes.
- If so, stop and see [Get help now](https://daynentang.com/en/get-help/).
