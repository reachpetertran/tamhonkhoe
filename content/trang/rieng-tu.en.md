## Summary

- No cookies, no ads, no tracking or behavioural analytics.
- No accounts, no forms. We never ask for your name, email or phone number.
- The site runs no JavaScript and loads no fonts, images or code from other sites.

## Who can see what when you open a page

The site is stored and delivered by Cloudflare. Like any web server, Cloudflare receives the technical information needed to send you the page, such as your IP address and browser type. Cloudflare handles that data under the [Cloudflare privacy policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/).

- [Verified] The pages contain no analytics code, and the security headers block any script from running on the page. Cloudflare Web Analytics is not enabled. (Checked on 4 November 2026.)
- [Inference] Cloudflare still records aggregate server-side statistics, such as number of visits and countries. We can only see those aggregate figures, not individual readers.
- [Verified] The site sends Cloudflare's default "network error logging" setting. If your browser supports it, it may report to Cloudflare when it fails to connect to the site.

## Links to other sites

Passages link to their original sources (SuttaCentral, eBible.org, Project Gutenberg…), and "Get help now" links to daynentang.com. When you follow a link, that site's own policy applies. This site passes on the minimum referrer information (the domain only).

## Articles about faith

Reading an article about a tradition does not mean you follow it. We do not know, store or ask which articles you read.
