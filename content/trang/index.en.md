## Who this site is for

Adults who want to read the original scriptures for themselves and practise a little each day. Each article has a short "Kids' corner 6+" to do together with a child, always with an adult present.

The site is written in Vietnamese first. Every article also has this English version; the Vietnamese version is the original.

## Three ways to start

- **Convergence**: practices that many traditions share, such as pausing, attention, gratitude and the Golden Rule. Each week has a section "Where the traditions differ", because the traditions are not the same.
- **Traditions**: Buddhism, Christianity, Islam, Judaism, Stoicism, Non-duality. Each tradition is presented in its own words, without ranking one above another.
- **I need help now**: if you are in crisis, this site is not the right place. Please open the Get help page on daynentang.com.

## How we work

- Scripture passages come from originals with a clear licence, with links so you can check them yourself. See [Sources and licences](/en/sources-and-licences/).
- Traditional teaching and scientific findings are kept apart and labelled. See [Evidence](/en/evidence/).
- An article about one religion goes live only after someone from that tradition has reviewed it. See [About and principles](/en/about/).
- The full content plan: [Courses](/en/courses/).
- No ads, no cookies, no tracking. See [Privacy](/en/privacy/).
