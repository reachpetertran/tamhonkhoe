## Mission

Free self-study of the spiritual life through the original scriptures of many traditions, presented fairly and honest about the evidence.

## Who it is for

- Adult self-learners. Each article has a "Kids' corner 6+" section of 5–10 minutes, as a game, always with an adult present.
- The Vietnamese version is the original. Every article also has an English version under /en/. The site never switches language automatically; each page has a link to the other language.

## What this site does and does not do

| We do | We do not |
|---|---|
| Explain a tradition in its own words: "This tradition teaches…" | Tell you what you should believe |
| Place traditions side by side on shared practices, and state clearly where they differ | Say "all religions are the same", or rank traditions |
| State clearly what scientific research has and has not found | Give medical advice, diagnosis or treatment |
| Link to the original source of every passage | Discuss politics or the history of religious conflict |

Crisis support, helplines, safety plans and short self-help skills (naming feelings, 1–3 minute breathing) have their home on [daynentang.com](https://daynentang.com/en/). Here we only link to them.

## Five questions before an article goes live

Every new article starts in the internal area. It goes live only when the answer to all five questions is "yes":

1. **Descriptive, not judgemental**: written as "this tradition teaches…", never "you should believe…".
2. **Not divisive**: no ranking, no criticism of any religion, belief or group, no politics.
3. **Clean sources**: every passage checked against the original, licence recorded.
4. **Reviewed**, according to how sensitive the course is (see below).
5. **Safe**: includes a "Stop practising if" section, no medical advice, no fasting or dieting in the kids' corner.

### Who reviews, by sensitivity

| Level | Includes | Reviewed by |
|---|---|---|
| Less sensitive | Content that does not set out the doctrine or ritual of one religion: core pages, the curriculum outline, Stoicism courses, Body (slow eating, movement) | The project owner. The page says clearly that no reviewer from a tradition has checked it |
| Needs someone from the tradition | Courses about one religion; Convergence courses | At least one reviewer from within that tradition; Convergence needs two reviewers from two traditions. Exception: Convergence 0, Convergence 1, the courses Buddhism 1 "Mindfulness and kindness", Buddhism 2 "The four foundations of mindfulness in 8 weeks" and Buddhism 4 "Dhamma in everyday life" were published early by decision of the project owner; each lesson says clearly that no reviewer from a tradition has checked it yet, and it will be reviewed again once reviewers join |
| Drafted internally first | Level 3 (God, self, death), deep doctrinal comparison, detailed fasting, content still being debated | Goes live only after a suitable reviewer. Exception: the course Stoicism 3 "Change and death in Stoicism" was published by decision of the project owner; it does not quote the passages where the Stoic writers discuss leaving life by one’s own choice, its kids’ corner is only about change in nature, and each lesson says clearly that no reviewer from a tradition has checked it yet. The course Buddhism 3 "Impermanence and not-self" was also published by decision of the project owner; it only describes Buddhist teaching and does not compare it with other traditions’ views of the soul or the self, does not quote the passages contemplating the body left behind, gives safety guidance about feeling detached from oneself, its kids’ corner is only about change, and each lesson says clearly that no reviewer from a tradition has checked it yet |

The other four questions (descriptive, not divisive, clean sources, safe) apply to every article at every level. Unverified claims never go live.

The Vietnamese and English versions of an article go live together, and only when both have passed. When in doubt, an article stays internal. Every change of this kind is recorded in the [Changelog](/en/changelog/).

## Where the passages come from

On the Vietnamese pages, for each passage we follow this order and stop at the first step that works:

1. A free-licensed Vietnamese translation exists: use it verbatim, with translator, licence and link.
2. If not: tamhonkhoe translates from a free-licensed English translation, says so clearly, and links to the English text. A reviewer checks the meaning.
3. The source is in copyright or may not be modified: quote very briefly or only link, and explain in our own words.

On the English pages, passages from English translations are quoted verbatim from the original, not translated back from Vietnamese.

Each article has at most three passages. The full list is on [Sources and licences](/en/sources-and-licences/).

## Labels in articles

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| [Traditional teaching] | Taken from the scriptures, not mixed with science |
| [Verified] | Based on a source we have opened and read |
| [Inference] | Drawn from a source, but the source does not say it directly |
| [Expert judgement] | The author's own assessment |

## Authors and reviewers

The site was started by Peter Tran as a non-profit self-study project. Reviewers are listed by role (for example "Buddhist reviewer 1"), never with personal details.

## The role of AI

To be clear: AI (Anthropic's Claude assistant) has contributed a great deal to building this site. AI wrote the website code, drafted the first versions of articles, translated passages from English into Vietnamese, wrote the English versions, and searched for scientific research.

What AI does not decide:

- **Sources and licences**: Peter opens the original page, reads the terms and confirms before any source is used.
- **The meaning of passages**: translations must be checked by someone from that tradition.
- **Going live**: an article goes live only when Peter agrees, and, where required, reviewers from the tradition too, according to the five questions above.

AI can be wrong. If you see a mistranslated passage, a wrong figure, or a presentation that is not fair, please let us know; every correction is recorded in the [Changelog](/en/changelog/).
