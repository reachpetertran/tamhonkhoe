This page collects every sourced claim on tamhonkhoe.com in two tables: one for science, one for scripture. The two kinds of source are kept apart because they answer different questions.

Labels used on the site:

- [Verified]: based on a source we have opened and read, named alongside.
- [Inference]: drawn from a source, but the source does not say it directly.
- [Expert judgement]: the author's own assessment, without a dedicated study.
Claims without a reliable enough source are marked "needs verification" in drafts and do not appear on this page.

## Table 1. What science finds

|---|---|---|---|
