Kids’ corner 6+

Twelve short games that go with the 12 Convergence weeks. Each takes 5–10 minutes, always with an adult playing too. No fasting, no dieting, no talk of trauma or death.

Kids’ corner – Activity 1: The breathing statue

Move around, freeze like a statue, count 3 breaths and say one thing you notice. Goes with the Convergence week "Pause".

Kids’ corner – Activity 2: Who helped me today?

Name three things: someone who helped, something in nature, something the body can do; then draw a thank-you card. Goes with the Convergence week "Gratitude".

Kids’ corner – Activity 3: Swapping shoes

Draw a shoe, swap drawings, then imagine "walking in someone else’s shoes" in a small situation. Goes with the Convergence week "The Golden Rule".

Kids’ corner – Activity 4: A pebble a day

Choose one small daily task; each time it is done, drop a pebble in a jar and count them at the end of the week. Goes with the Convergence week "Discipline".

Kids’ corner – Activity 5: The muddy jar

Shake a jar of water with a little soil until it clouds, then sit still together and watch it clear. Goes with the Convergence week "Rest".

Kids’ corner – Activity 6: A kind deed at home

Pick someone at home who might need help and secretly do one small thing for them. Goes with the Convergence week "Service".

Kids’ corner – Activity 7: Sending good wishes

Choose three people or creatures to wish well, then say or draw one wish for each. Goes with the Convergence week "Loving-kindness".

Kids’ corner – Activity 8: Making up

After a small quarrel, once everyone is calm, each child says how they felt and together they find a fix. Goes with the Convergence week "Forgiveness".

Kids’ corner – Activity 9: Water flows low

Drip water onto a tilted tray, watch it run to the low end, then choose one "water-like" thing to do. Goes with the Convergence week "Letting go of self".

Kids’ corner – Activity 10: Falling leaves

Collect green, yellow and brown leaves, line them up by colour and talk about things that change. Only about change in nature. Goes with the Convergence week "Accepting change".

Kids’ corner – Activity 11: Listening to the bell fade

Strike a bell gently, listen as the sound fades, and raise a hand when it is gone. Goes with the Convergence week "Silence".

Kids’ corner – Activity 12: A small gift

Choose someone to receive a hand-drawn card or a toy the child wants to give, then deliver it together. Goes with the Convergence week "Giving and review".
