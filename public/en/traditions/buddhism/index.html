Buddhism

Buddhism 1 – Week 1: Sitting with the breath

The Discourse on Mindfulness of Breathing begins with sitting down and knowing the in-breath and the out-breath. Sit for 10 minutes a day, just knowing the breath.

Buddhism 1 – Week 2: The four truths in your own words

By tradition, the Buddha’s first discourse: the middle way and the four noble truths. Read slowly, then rewrite them in your own words.

Buddhism 1 – Week 3: Speech

Two Dhammapada verses on defeating anger with kindness and speaking the truth. Each day, pause for one breath before speaking, and practise speech that is true and gentle.

Buddhism 1 – Week 4: Non-harm

All tremble at the rod, all love life: treat others like yourself. Each day, choose and carry out one deliberate act of non-harm.

Buddhism 1 – Week 5: Loving-kindness

The Discourse on Love: live uprightly and gently, then unfold a heart of love to all beings. Ten minutes a day of good wishes in widening circles.

Buddhism 1 – Week 6: Testing for yourself

The discourse to the Kālāmas: when you know for yourselves what leads to harm and what leads to welfare. The last week looks back at five habits using your own notes.
