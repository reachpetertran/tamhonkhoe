Islam

Islam 1 – Week 1: The Opening

Al-Fatihah, the first chapter of the Qur'an, seven short verses. Ten minutes of slow reading a day: choose one word and write one line. The course describes; it does not teach ritual.

Islam 1 – Week 2: The five pillars (described)

The five pillars of Islam as described in the hadith of Jibril, and Qur'an 2:177: righteousness is not turning your faces to the East and the West. Described, not taught as ritual.

Islam 1 – Week 3: Gratitude

Qur'an 14:7 and 14:34: "If ye give thanks, I will give you more"; blessings too many to count. And a hadith: whoever does not thank people is not thankful to Allah. Note one thing you are grateful for each evening; thank two people this week.

Islam 1 – Week 4: Patience, within one's capacity

Chapter 103 (Al-'Asr): "exhort one another to endurance"; and Qur'an 2:286: "Allah tasketh not a soul beyond its scope." Each day, sort what is within and beyond your capacity, and take one small step on a hard task.

Islam 1 – Week 5: That you may know one another

Qur'an 49:13: people were made nations and tribes "that ye may know one another". One conversation to understand someone from another culture, region or generation.

Islam 1 – Week 6: Small regular deeds (and review)

Qur'an 99:7–8: whoever does good an atom's weight will see it; and the hadith Bukhari 6464: the deed most beloved to Allah is the one done regularly, even if small. Keep one small deed on 6 of 7 days, then review the six weeks.
