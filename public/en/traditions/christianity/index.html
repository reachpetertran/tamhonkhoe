Christianity

Christianity 1 – Week 1: The shepherd

Psalm 23: "The LORD is my shepherd; I shall lack nothing." Getting to know the four steps of lectio divina: reading, meditation, response, stillness. Ten minutes of slow reading a day.

Christianity 1 – Week 2: The Beatitudes

Matthew 5:3–9: "Blessed are the peacemakers." Slowly reading the seven opening lines of the Sermon on the Mount in four steps, and writing one line after each reading.

Christianity 1 – Week 3: The Lord’s Prayer (described)

Matthew 6:9–13, the prayer Jesus taught his disciples. The lesson describes it and reads it slowly line by line; praying it is optional, for those who believe.

Christianity 1 – Week 4: The Good Samaritan

Luke 10:25–37: "Who is my neighbor?" Reading the parable of the Samaritan slowly in four steps, then during the week helping a stranger once, within safe limits.

Christianity 1 – Week 5: Do not worry

Matthew 6:25–34: "See the birds of the sky… Consider the lilies of the field." Slow reading in four steps; once in the week, write down three worries and reread the passage. This lesson is not a treatment for anxiety.

Christianity 1 – Week 6: What love is (and review)

1 Corinthians 13:4–7: "Love is patient and is kind." Choose one quality to practise all week, then rate the six weeks of slow reading and keep two habits.

Christianity 2 – Week 1: Presence in ordinary work

Brother Lawrence, a kitchen friar in seventeenth-century Paris, taught that one can live in the presence of God in the middle of ordinary work. Each day, sit in silence for 10 minutes and do one small task fully.

Christianity 2 – Week 2: The inner life

The Imitation of Christ, Book II, chapter 1: the kingdom of God is within. Each day, sit in silence for 12 minutes and notice when outward things pull you away.

Christianity 2 – Week 3: Go into your room

Matthew 6:5–8: praying in secret, without many words; and Brother Lawrence's advice on wandering thoughts. Each day, sit in silence for 15 minutes in the same place.

Christianity 2 – Week 4: A quieted soul

Psalm 131, three short verses about a soul stilled like a weaned child with its mother. Each day, sit in silence for 15 minutes, building up to 20.

Christianity 2 – Week 5: A short prayer

Luke 18:9–14, the parable of the Pharisee and the tax collector. The tax collector's one-sentence prayer is the root of many short prayers in the Christian tradition. Each day, sit in silence for 20 minutes with one short sentence.

Christianity 2 – Week 6: A daily rhythm of prayer

Brother Lawrence: no great matters, only a little lifting up of the heart from time to time; and Paul's "pray without ceasing". Sit in silence for 20 minutes each morning, pause three times during the day, look back in the evening.

Christianity 2 – Week 7: Dryness and perseverance

The Imitation of Christ II.9 and Brother Lawrence on times when prayer feels dry and empty of feeling. Each day, sit in silence for 20 minutes, dry days included. The lesson also explains how dryness differs from depression.

Christianity 2 – Week 8: Review

Brother Lawrence: serving in freedom, without forcing oneself. In the final week, sit in silence for 25 minutes, rate the course's 7 habits, keep 2 and write a 4-week plan.

Christianity 3 – Week 1: The cloud of unknowing

The Cloud of Unknowing (14th century) teaches that in prayer one does not grasp God with the understanding, but meets only a darkness, a "cloud of unknowing". Each day, 25 minutes of silent sitting without trying to picture anything.

Christianity 3 – Week 2: Loved, but not thought

The Cloud of Unknowing chapters 5 and 6: put a "cloud of forgetting" beneath you, because God "may well be loved, but not thought". Each day, 25 minutes of silent sitting, letting thoughts pass without following them.

Christianity 3 – Week 3: One short word

The Cloud of Unknowing chapter 7: wrap your intent towards God in one very short word, such as "God" or "love", and return to that word whenever you are pulled away. Each day, 30 minutes of silent sitting with one word.

Christianity 3 – Week 4: The divine darkness

The Mystical Theology, chapter 1, by the author writing as Dionysius: a prayer to be led into the "darkness" beyond light and knowledge, and the story of Moses on the mountain. Each day, notice one image you hold of something vast, then sit in silence for 30 minutes.

Christianity 3 – Week 5: Silence before mystery

The Mystical Theology chapter 3: the higher we rise, the fewer our words, until there is complete silence. The Cloud of Unknowing chapter 46: do not strain, be gentle. The longest practice of the course, 40 minutes, and one reading with another person.

Christianity 3 – Week 6: Review

Review the course with three passages: The Divine Names chapter 1 (speak of God only what Scripture reveals), The Mystical Theology chapter 5 (He is beyond every affirmation and negation) and The Cloud of Unknowing chapter 6. Sit for 30–40 minutes, rate six habits, keep two.
