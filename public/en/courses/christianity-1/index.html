Slow reading of Scripture (lectio divina)

Psalm 23, the Beatitudes, the Lord’s Prayer, the parable of the Samaritan, "the birds and the lilies" and 1 Corinthians 13, read slowly in the four steps of lectio divina: reading, meditation, response, stillness; from week 4, one small action is added.

What science finds

  • 97 studies, 7,181 people: therapy that integrates the client’s faith reduces distress as well as standard therapy and adds spiritual well-being. There is no review of lectio divina itself. (Captari et al. 2018, Journal of Clinical Psychology 74(11) · source)
  • Meta-analysis of randomised trials: religious/spiritual interventions reduce anxiety; programmes vary widely and study quality is limited. (Gonçalves et al. 2015, Psychological Medicine 45(14) · source)
  • 53 studies, 2,323 people: guided forgiveness programmes clearly increase forgiveness compared with no treatment, and reduce depression and anxiety; longer programmes work better. (Wade et al. 2014, Journal of Consulting and Clinical Psychology 82(1) · source)
  • 27 experiments, 4,045 people: acts of kindness raise well-being by a small-to-moderate amount; the studies are often small. (Curry et al. 2018, Journal of Experimental Social Psychology 76 · source)
  • Review of 20 meta-analyses: Acceptance and Commitment Therapy (ACT) works for anxiety, depression and chronic pain, about as well as CBT. This is guided therapy. (Gloster et al. 2020, Journal of Contextual Behavioral Science 18 · source)
  • 33 studies of self-control training: a small effect, smaller still after correcting for publication bias. (Friese et al. 2017, Perspectives on Psychological Science 12(6) · source)

Limits, honestly

A Level 1 course: about 12–15 minutes a day. The course describes and practises the Christian tradition’s way of slow reading; learners do not need to be Christian, and the ‘response’ step offers an option for those who do not pray (writing one sentence in response), which is tamhonkhoe’s adaptation rather than a form of the tradition. The English pages quote the World English Bible. Lesson 3 does not invite anyone to pray in a faith they do not hold. The lines on forgiving and ‘enduring all things’ are not for harm from violence, abuse or control. Lesson 5 is not a treatment for anxiety, nor financial or medical advice. No reviewer from within the Christian tradition has checked it yet, and there is no review of lectio divina itself; the evidence in the lessons is only indirect.

Stop practising if

Stop or shorten practice and talk to a professional if you notice: rising anxiety or panic; low mood that lingers; feeling that you or the world is ‘not real’; flooding memories of trauma; several nights of poor sleep; hearing or seeing things others do not; thoughts of harming yourself. People with a history of psychosis, bipolar disorder or severe PTSD should ask a doctor before practising more than 20 minutes a day.
