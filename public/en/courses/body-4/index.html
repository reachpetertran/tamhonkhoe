What science finds

  • 11 studies, 1,436 people: stress and anxiety scores were lower after Ramadan than before, but most studies had no control group. (Berthelot et al. 2021, Nutrients 13(11) · source)
  • Review: Ramadan’s effect on mental health is generally small, but people with schizophrenia or bipolar disorder have a higher risk of relapse. (Heun 2018, Global Psychiatry 1(1) · source)

Limits, honestly

The traditions themselves exempt the sick, pregnant women, children and travellers. The course only describes; it does not teach fasting.

Stop practising if

Stop or shorten practice and talk to a professional if you notice: rising anxiety or panic; low mood that lingers; feeling that you or the world is ‘not real’; flooding memories of trauma; several nights of poor sleep; hearing or seeing things others do not; thoughts of harming yourself. People with a history of psychosis, bipolar disorder or severe PTSD should ask a doctor before practising more than 20 minutes a day.
