Body

Body 1 – Week 1: Grace before meals

Deuteronomy 8:10, Matthew 14:19 and Qur'an 2:172 speak of giving thanks when eating. Each day, pause for one minute before one meal and say or think a thank-you in your own words.

Body 1 – Week 2: One slow meal

The Discourse on Mindfulness Meditation (MN 10) teaches awareness when eating, drinking, chewing and tasting; chapter 12 of the Tao Te Ching says the five flavours take away the mouth's taste. Each day, eat one meal slowly over about 15 minutes.

Body 1 – Week 3: A screen-free meal

Pirkei Avot, the Acts of the Apostles and Ecclesiastes speak of shared meals with good words and gladness. This week, have at least three meals without screens, with other people if you can.

Body 1 – Week 4: Thanking growers and cooks

Qur'an 80:24–32 invites people to consider their food; the Talmud tells how Ben Zoma remembered all the effort behind a loaf of bread. This week, say one specific thank-you to someone who made your food, then review the course.

Body 2 – Week 1: Walking meditation

The Discourse on Mindfulness Meditation (MN 10) teaches knowing that you are walking, standing, sitting or lying down; chapter 64 of the Tao Te Ching says the journey of a thousand li begins with a single step. Each day, walk very slowly for 10 minutes.

Body 2 – Week 2: Slow stretching

The Discourse on Mindfulness Meditation (MN 10) teaches awareness when bending and extending the limbs; 1 Corinthians 6:19–20 calls the body a temple. Each day, stretch slowly for 10 minutes, never into pain.

Body 2 – Week 3: Flowing like water

Chapter 78 of the Tao Te Ching says nothing is softer and weaker than water; Isaiah 40:29–31 speaks of those who run and are not weary; Seneca recommends short, simple exercises and a quick return to the mind. Each day, join stretching and walking into one 15-minute session.

Body 2 – Week 4: Review and a rhythm of your own

Epictetus (Encheiridion XLI) and 1 Timothy 4:8 put bodily exercise in second place; a hadith in Sahih al-Bukhari reminds us that the body has its rights. Review the three weeks, rate yourself and choose a rhythm of movement for next month.
